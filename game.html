Instructions

The goal of the game is to survive and destroy the enemy base.

The game is divided in 5 ages. To move to the next age you need XP. You gain XP by killing enemy units, and also (less) when one of your units is killed. You can also build defences. Finding the balance between defence and offence is the key.

You have a special attack with its own cooldown; each age has a different one. You cannot repair your base, but it gains health every time you evolve. Protect your base at all cost!

  • Mouse: click the menu buttons. Move to the screen edges to scroll. The ◻ / ▭ button switches between the classic 2000s view and a wide view.
  • Touch: tap buttons, drag the battlefield or hold ◀ ▶ to scroll.
  • Keys: Space pause · Esc cancel · ← → scroll · 1–5 menu buttons

PAUSED

Press space again or tap to resume
